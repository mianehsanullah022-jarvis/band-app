# Band

My own iPhone app for my fitness band.

- **Home**: clean dark dashboard. Live heart rate and battery when the band shares them; other cards are marked *Sample* until decoded.
- **Detective**: finds the band over Bluetooth, lists everything it offers, listens to it and saves a log to share.

## Get it on the iPhone (free)
1. Open **Releases → latest** and download `Band.ipa`.
2. On the desktop, open **Sideloadly**, plug in the iPhone, drop in `Band.ipa`, sign in with your Apple ID, press **Start**.
3. On the iPhone: **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management** → trust your Apple ID.
4. Turn on **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode** if asked.

With a free Apple ID, reinstall every 7 days.

Built for free by GitHub Actions on every push to `main`.
